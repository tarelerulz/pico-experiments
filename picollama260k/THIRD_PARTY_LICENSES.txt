This project's own work is the RP2040 port: flash-backed weight loading
(replacing mmap/fread), USB-serial I/O (replacing stdio), and capping the
context length so the KV-cache fits in 264KB of SRAM. The transformer
inference code itself (structs, forward pass, tokenizer, sampler, generate)
in picollama.c is verbatim from Andrej Karpathy's llama2.c, and the model
weights in model_data.h are his published stories260K checkpoint, not
trained by this project.
